About the organization

The Early Music Literacy Program helps young children discover the basics of music in a way that feels enjoyable and welcoming.

What we believe

Every child should have the chance to explore music, even if they have never played an instrument or taken a lesson.

Children should be able to listen, experiment, create sounds, and express themselves without worrying about being judged. Music is not only something to perform correctly. It is something to explore, enjoy, imagine, and create.

That is why nothing on this site is a test. There are no scores, no levels, no timers, and no wrong answers.

What children meet here

  • Beat
  • Rhythm
  • Melody
  • Pitch
  • Tempo
  • Loud and soft
  • High and low
  • Instrument families
  • Patterns
  • Listening
  • Musical memory
  • Creative expression

How we work

  • We start from play. Children tap, listen, compare, and make things before they learn the names for them.
  • We describe, we do not grade. "That sound is higher" instead of "correct."
  • We keep it open. Every activity can be repeated, changed, and restarted in any order.
  • We keep it honest. Music is a valuable creative and educational experience. We do not promise medical or academic results.
  • We keep children's privacy. The play areas collect no personal information at all.

Who is behind it

The program is being developed with student volunteers connected to the Fairfax County, Virginia chapter of The Back to BACH Project, a student-led nonprofit that brings free music and arts presentations to children.

Young musicians lead the sessions and share the instruments they play. This website carries the same idea online, so any child can start exploring at home or at school.

Founding members

Four young musicians started this program. They lead the sessions and share the instruments they play.
