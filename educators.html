For schools and educators

Four ready-to-use lesson outlines for pre-K to grade 3. Each takes 20 to 30 minutes, needs no instruments, and pairs with an activity on this site.

Lesson outlines

Lesson 1: Find the steady beat

Children will feel and keep a steady beat with their bodies, and hear the difference between beat and rhythm.

You need open floor space, and one screen with sound for Feel the Beat.

  1. Warm up (5 min). Children pat their knees to your steady count. Try it slow, then a little faster.
  2. Listen (5 min). Start the beat on screen at Walking speed. Children march in place to it.
  3. Explore (10 min). Take turns tapping the big circle. Ask: what changed when you tapped near the edge?
  4. Create (5 min). In pairs, one child keeps the beat on their knees while the other claps any rhythm.
  5. Share. "What did you notice about the beat?" Accept every answer.
Lesson 2: High and low

Children will describe sounds as higher or lower and show pitch with their bodies.

You need one screen with sound for Explore Pitch.

  1. Warm up (5 min). Voice slides: follow your hand up and down with an "ooo" sound.
  2. Listen (5 min). Walk up the musical stairs on screen. Children rise from crouching to tiptoe.
  3. Explore (10 min). A child taps two steps. The class shows with their hands whether the second sound went up or down. Describe, do not correct.
  4. Create (5 min). Children draw a line that goes up and down, then sing or hum it.
Lesson 3: Instrument families

Children will name four ways instruments make sound: hitting, shaking, plucking or bowing, and blowing.

You need one screen with sound for Instrument World, and a few classroom objects.

  1. Listen (10 min). Play each instrument on screen. After each one ask, "How do you think the player makes that sound?"
  2. Sort (5 min). Use the family buttons to group the instruments. Mime playing each one.
  3. Explore (10 min). Sound hunt: children find one classroom object to tap and one to shake.
  4. Share. Each child shows their sound. The class echoes it with their voices.
Lesson 4: Make a class melody

Children will build a short melody together and hear how changing notes or tempo changes the music.

You need one screen with sound for Make a Melody.

  1. Warm up (5 min). Echo singing: you sing three notes on "la", children copy.
  2. Create (15 min). Each child chooses one colorful note to add. Play the melody after every few notes.
  3. Explore (5 min). Change the instrument. Change the speed. Ask what feels different.
  4. Share. Give the melody a name together. There is no wrong melody.
