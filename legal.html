3. Children's privacy

We built the play areas to follow the spirit of the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting personal information from children in the first place.

  • No accounts. Children never enter a name, email, age, photo, or password.
  • No tracking. The site has no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking cookies.
  • No saved creations. Rhythms, melodies, and drawings stay on the screen and disappear when the page is closed. We never receive them.
  • Two small settings stay on your device. The browser remembers (a) the date an adult signed off and (b) the Reduce motion choice. They are stored only in that browser, are never sent to us, and can be removed with the button at the bottom of this page.
  • The birth year in the sign-off is checked on your device and then discarded. It is not stored or sent.
  • Email. If an adult chooses to email us, we use that email only to reply. Please do not send a child's medical or other sensitive information by email.

Like every website, the company that hosts the site may keep standard technical logs (for example, IP address and time of request) for security. We do not use them to identify children.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. We are not responsible for the content or practices of other websites. We recommend that adults watch videos together with children.

5. Safe listening and comfort

  • Volume. Sounds on this site are limited in loudness, but your device's volume is under your control. Start low and keep it comfortable. Take extra care with headphones.
  • Motion and light. The site uses gentle animation. A Reduce motion button is on every page. If a child feels dizzy, unwell, or uncomfortable, stop and take a break.
  • Breaks. Short sessions are best for young children.
  • Movement. Some ideas invite children to march, clap, or dance. Make sure there is safe space to move.
